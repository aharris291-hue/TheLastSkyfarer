# Open Decisions (Phase 1, awaiting Adam's approval)

Each item gives options and a recommendation (**Rec**). Nothing here is treated as canon until Adam approves it; approved items move to [DECISION_LOG.md](DECISION_LOG.md). D-1 to D-11 are the Bible's open decisions named in the production prompt. D-12 onward are conflicts found while consolidating the sources.

## Bible open decisions

**D-1 The Hollow Tempest**
- A. The Hollow Tempest *is* Aeolus in his contained, wounded state: the storm at the heart of the Storm Wall is his body, hollowed out by the containment machinery. The Storm Wall is the outer ring. *(Matches the Outline codex: "never a monster. It was a wounded guardian.")*
- B. A separate wound in the Great Sky left by the Sundering. It feeds corruption into Aeolus, and healing him closes it.
- C. An artificial storm generated by the containment engines that keeps Aeolus asleep.
- **Rec A.** It adds no new lore, keeps the finale a rescue, and makes the corruption nodes the containment machinery grafted onto him.

**D-2 How Piko is connected to Elias**
- A. Watcher: Piko observed Elias for years and never spoke. Elias sensed him, sketched him and talked to him anyway (as in the four flashbacks). After Elias died, Piko stayed near the Bluff and chose Kieran.
- B. Guide: Piko quietly steered Elias toward the glider and the Veilmist route, then waited for someone ready to finish the journey.
- C. Bound: Piko is tied to the ancient glider or compass, and Elias's discovery of it woke him.
- **Rec A**, with a light touch of B in Act III (Piko admits he left clues). It matches every Outline flashback and the line "He did. / Right. You knew him."

**D-3 The Sleeping Current encounter**
- A. Boss fight against an environmental guardian: strike exposed power nodes with the staff while dodging surges.
- B. No combat: a traversal and puzzle gauntlet that redirects wind streams to restart the engine.
- C. Hybrid in three beats: redirect wind streams (puzzle) → ride and dodge energy surges (traversal) → stabilise the engine by striking power nodes (light combat). There is no health bar on a creature; the engine's stability meter fills.
- **Rec C.** It uses every mechanic the Outline lists and teaches "restore, don't destroy" in the first boss-scale moment.

**D-4 How and when the Skyfarer's Staff is acquired**
- A. Chapter 2: it lies dormant in the workshop's hidden compartment with the glider and compass. Elias could never wake it. Its symbol first glows at the Veilmist marker.
- B. Chapter 4: found at Elias's secret camp on Veilmist, left there for whoever followed.
- C. Act II: Act I uses a plain courier's walking stick and the staff arrives with the Seekers.
- **Rec B.** It rewards the camp discovery (ties into Elias Was Here and Flashback I), puts the staff in hand before the Outline's marker scene and the Sleeping Current, and keeps the workshop focused on the glider and compass. Milestone A therefore has no staff and no combat.

**D-5 Combat spec**
- A. Light staff combat: 3-hit combo, dodge, parry, and a wind-push special. Enemies are corrupted wind creatures that are *calmed or cleansed* rather than killed. Built on the Gameplay Ability System; combat is rare and readable.
- B. Strictly non-lethal stun-and-cleanse with no combo system.
- C. Fuller action combat with weapon upgrades and many enemy types.
- **Rec A.** It fits PEGI 16 peril with no gore and the healing theme, and leaves room for the "most demanding" Tempest Ascendant phase.

**D-6 Glider controls and upgrades**
- A. Energy model (no stamina bar): dive to build speed, pull up to trade speed for height. Updrafts and wind currents add energy, the air-brake costs speed. Optional "assist" mode holds altitude for accessibility.
- B. Stamina meter that drains while gliding and refills on land.
- C. Arcade: constant speed and simple lift, no resource.
- **Rec A.** It makes reading the wind the skill, as the Bible asks. Recovery: dropping into the cloud sea, a wind gust returns you to the last safe ledge with a short fade and no other penalty. Upgrades (crafted at the workshop): handling, current-catch radius, air-brake, and later a Resonator boost.

**D-7 Skill tree**
- A. Three branches (Traversal, Explorer and Piko, Combat), with points earned from discoveries and restorations, never from kills.
- B. No tree; all growth comes from story items and crafting.
- C. Story abilities fixed (glider, staff modes, Resonator) plus a small perk tree (option A, about 15 perks).
- **Rec C.**

**D-8 Crafting**
- A. Light: gather materials while exploring and upgrade the glider, satchel and staff at the workshop bench. No consumable recipes.
- B. Full recipes including consumables and gear.
- C. No crafting in the Act I slice.
- **Rec A.** The Outline's side-quest rewards already fit it (satchel upgrade, rare crafting material).

**D-9 Master world map**
- A. Concentric. Brindle Isle at the centre of the known sky (the Outline map prompt calls it the "central region"). Inner ring: Mistreef/Veilmist and Verdant Reach. Middle ring: Ember Peaks and Azure Expanse. Outer ring: Shattered Sky and Starfall Basin. The Storm Wall circles everything, and the Lost Continent lies beyond it on the side the Veilmist route points to. Settlements: Oakspire (Verdant), Ashfall City (Ember), Tidehome (Azure), Horizon's End (Shattered Sky edge), Lumina's Rest (Starfall), and Aerion Prime as the Aerian capital and City of Winds on the Lost Continent.
- B. Linear chain of regions toward the Storm Wall.
- **Rec A.** A full layout with Skyroutes and Beacons comes in the Phase 1 world-map deliverable after approval.

## Conflicts found in the sources

**D-12 Piko's first speech (wording and timing)**
- **Rec:** Bible wording (5 lines), placed in Ch. 16 *Piko's Secret* at a ruin where a Skyfarer map symbol lights. Piko is non-verbal (vocalisations and body language) for all of Acts I–II. Outline banter in which Piko speaks before Ch. 16 is replaced by chirp and gesture versions; the spoken versions play only on later revisits. The Outline's extra tag ("You never asked.") is dropped unless you want it back.

**D-13 Piko's Notes before he speaks**
- A. Kieran writes them as his reading of Piko ("Piko stared at this marking like he'd seen it before"). After Ch. 16, notes switch to Piko's own voice and the early ones gain a Piko annotation.
- B. No Piko's Notes until Ch. 16.
- **Rec A.** It sets up a nice reveal on re-reading.

**D-14 Meeting Piko and the launch point**
- **Rec:** in Ch. 3 Piko is found sitting on the glider at the Bluff just before the first launch, "as if he's always belonged there" (the Outline key scene), and the launch is from the Bluff edge over the cloud sea. Cloud Edge stays Kieran's contemplative spot (Ch. 1 scene, later revisits). The Godot demo did the same.

**D-15 Opening and Kieran's home**
- A. Kieran has lived in a rented room in the village since Elias died, and the Bluff has been sealed pending the will. Opening: sunrise on the Upper Cliffs with "There has to be more out there than this.", then deliveries. The deed at sunset opens the Bluff. *(Keeps the Outline opening scene and the Bible's "inherits the workshop" chapter ending; the room detail is new and needs approval.)*
- B. Kieran still lives at the Bluff, but Elias's workshop wing was locked by Elias's will. Opening: stepping out of the house at sunrise (the Outline's later recommendation).
- **Rec A.**

**D-16 Veilmist questline (Milestone B)**

The Outline's objectives fill them: Q1 *The Impossible Route* = the Ch. 3 glide and arrival (the Godot demo's "Into the Clouds"); Q2 Mistfall Cliffs (three Aerian markers); Q3 Whispergrove (first Mist Sprites, key fragment 1); Q4 Crystal Hollow (reflection puzzles, fragment 2); Q5 Silent Basin (Piko finds the hidden passage, fragment 3); Q6 Forgotten Observatory (Wind Engines Alpha, Beta, Gamma); Q7 The Sleeping Current (D-3); Q8 The Wind Resonator (D-17); Q9 The First Skyfarer Message ("To those who find this place... the routes are failing."); Q10 Beacon Awakened, then the Cassian teaser.

**D-17 The Wind Resonator**

- B. Infrastructure only, no player ability.
- **Rec A.** It satisfies both sources.

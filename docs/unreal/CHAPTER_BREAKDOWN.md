# Story-to-Game Breakdown: 5 Acts, 30 Chapters (Phase 1)

Status: draft for Adam's review, 2026-11-02. Chapter titles and endings are the Bible's (section 16). Content marked (O) comes from the script outline. Acts II to V are planning-level only: each region gets a full quest script in Phase 10. Names the Bible does not have (O-only bosses such as the Rootwarden) are candidates, not canon, until Adam approves them per region.

Banter triggers use the Bible's list: R = entering a region, L = major landmark, A = ancient record, S = side quest done, B = Beacon, W = dangerous weather, V = revisiting a familiar place, M = story milestone. Before Ch. 16 Piko's banter is non-verbal (D-12).

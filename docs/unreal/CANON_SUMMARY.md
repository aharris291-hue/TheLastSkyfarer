## 1. Premise

Kieran Thorn, a 17-year-old courier apprentice from Windrest Village on Brindle Isle, inherits his late grandfather Elias's workshop and finds an ancient glider, a brass compass and Elias's journals. With Piko, a mischievous cloud-spirit who is secretly an Aether Guardian, he uncovers the truth of the Sundering and must heal Aeolus, Guardian of the Great Sky, rather than destroy it. **[B+O]**

Creative north star: Kieran and Piko travelling together through a vast sky, rediscovering forgotten places and becoming responsible for reconnecting a broken world. **[B]**

Priority order (cut from the bottom, never the top): Kieran's journey → Kieran and Piko → gliding and exploration → the Sundering mystery → Beacons and routes → the Cassian conflict → healing Aeolus. **[B]**

Guardian creed: **Observe. Guide. Preserve. Endure.** Tenets: Balance Before Power, Patience Before Action, Stewardship Before Ownership. Guardians preserve; they do not rule. **[B+O]**

## 2. Characters

### Kieran Thorn (protagonist) [B+O]
- 17, courier apprentice, Windrest Village. Raised by Elias in the workshop on Old Cartographer's Bluff **[O]**.
- Curious, optimistic, adventurous, a little impulsive, funny, slightly vulnerable. An ordinary kid, not a chosen one or warrior **[B+O]**.
- Arc: curiosity → wonder → recognising loss → moral complexity of the Sundering → conflict with Cassian → accepting responsibility. Ends as "the first of a new generation" of Skyfarers, not the last **[B+O]**.
- Look **[O, matches Adam's turnaround sheet]**: Black teen, medium-dark brown skin, warm hazel-brown eyes, thick natural curly black hair (longer on top, windswept), lean athletic build. Weathered olive-green explorer jacket (rolled sleeves, aged brass buttons), cream linen shirt, brown travel trousers with reinforced knees, worn leather boots, fingerless gloves, leather courier satchel worn across the body, map tube diagonally on his back, brass compass on his belt, vintage flight goggles around his neck (worn in flight), and Elias's frayed **sky-blue scarf**, his signature silhouette element.
- Palette **[O]**: jacket #5C6E4F, scarf #4B9FE1, shirt #E9E1CC, trousers #725744, leather #8B5A2B, brass #C79A3B.
- Expressions **[O]**: neutral, happy, curious, mischievous, determined, shocked, awe and wonder.

### Piko (companion, hidden Aether Guardian) [B+O]
- House-cat sized (16–18 in sitting), can sit on Kieran's shoulder. Soft rounded body, slightly elongated torso, head about 40% of mass, short athletic legs, floating spirit tail about 1.5× body length ending in wisps of light. Original blend of fox, rabbit and flying squirrel **[O]**.
- Ivory-white cloud-like fur (#F5F4EF, shadow #D8DCE2), sky-blue (#87C7FF) and silver accents, pale-cyan markings (#ADEFFF), large golden-amber eyes (#F4BC42, highlight #FFD970), long feather-like ears with blue gradient tips **[O]**.
- Markings, faint at first, a "map of the wind" **[O]**: Heart Compass (8-point wind-blade compass, chest), Horizon Crest (forehead), Wind Routes (ears), Skyfarer's Path (constellation line down the spine), Twin Isles symbol (split across shoulders), Current Lines (legs, leave light trails), Wayfinder Spiral (tail, dissolving into star particles). In Guardian form all glow cyan, gold and white and connect into **the Great Sky Chart** **[B+O]**.
- Personality: curious, patient, observant (Kieran rushes, Piko notices). Mischievous: steals small objects, disrupts deliveries, hoards shiny things, sleeps in the satchel, chases seeds, mimics villagers, sits on important documents **[B+O]**.
- Communicates through chirps, trills, whistles, ear and tail language until his delayed **first speech** **[B+O]**.
- Centuries old; connected to Elias **[B]**. Watched Elias from afar for years; Elias glimpsed him; Piko appears in Elias's sketches **[O]**.
- Gameplay: small openings, distant switches, item retrieval, pointing out secrets, reacting to Aerian tech, detecting hidden wind routes, identifying Skyfarer landmarks, journal observations ("Piko's Notes") **[B+O]**. Must help without solving puzzles for the player **[B]**.
- Arc: playful and evasive → protective → the burden of watching the world forget → hope **[O]**.

### Elias Thorn (deceased grandfather) [B+O]
Cartographer and explorer, considered eccentric, "closer to the truth than anyone realized". His workshop holds journals (24 plus a final chapter **[O]**), maps, instruments and the ancient glider. Revealed through journals and four flashbacks (Veilmist, Ember Peaks, Azure Expanse, Starfall Basin) **[O]**. Gentle, wry, wise.

### Cassian Vale (Grand Seeker, ideological rival) [B+O]
Leads the Seekers. Lost family as a child trying to travel between islands **[O]**; believes the world was made into a cage. Charismatic, intelligent, credible and initially a real ally. His flaw is his growing willingness to decide the world's future for everyone. Turns at the collapsed-bridge confrontation, recognises Kieran may be right, but believes it is too late to stop.

### Windrest residents
- **Maren Holt**: postmaster of the Post House, practical, organised, stern but caring; early mentor **[O]** (Bible lists her only as a supporting NPC).
- **Tessa Reed**: childhood friend, Kieran's age, clever, competitive, sceptical, later one of his strongest allies **[O]**.
- **Rowan Finch**: village elder, keeps traditions, thinks the old stories should stay stories **[O only]**.
- No names in either document for the tavern keeper, flower grower or other villagers (see Open Decisions).

### Wider cast (roles from NPC packages) [B+O]
Sage Alder (Oakspire, Verdant Reach) · Rhea Vulkan and Kael Ashforge (Ashfall City, Ember Peaks; young Rhea appears in an Elias flashback) · Admiral Tidecrest (Tidehome, Azure Expanse) · Lysette Vale and Elder Thalen (Lumina's Rest, Starfall) · Archive Caretaker Projection (Aerion Prime, Lost Continent).

### Aeolus, Guardian of the Great Sky [B+O]
About 500 ft, a blend of wind dragon, sky whale and living storm, made of cloud, light, wind and ancient energy. Wounded, with corruption nodes; maintains weather, routes and migrations. The Sundering contained "instability associated with Aeolus". A rescue, not a kill.

### Factions
- **Seekers** [B+O]: Grand Seeker (Cassian), Executive Circle, Historical Recovery, Cartographic, Engineering and Exploration divisions. Independent, government-funded. Split late in Act III into Cassian supporters and Moderates; many leave him after Act IV begins.
- **Aerians / Aerian Commonwealth**: built the Skyroute Network, Beacons, wind engines, observatories and archives; "thought in centuries"; chose the Sundering. Neither evil nor infallible.
- **Skyfarers**: explorers and route keepers. Kieran becomes the first recognised Skyfarer in generations.

## 3. Places

### Brindle Isle and Windrest Village (Act I hub) [O, Bible names only]
Lush, not wealthy, "simply home". Rolling hills, windmills, orchards, flower-covered cliffs, steady winds. Population 150–200. Architecture: stone paths, wooden homes, flower boxes, colourful roofs, hanging lanterns, community gardens.

| Location | Canon detail |
|---|---|
| Central Square | Heart of the village and quest hub: bulletin board, fountain, market stalls |
| Post House | Courier HQ and Kieran's workplace; where he gets his first tasks |
| Windsong Tavern | Social centre, weather talk and legends; most adults dismiss Skyfarers as fairy tales |
| Whispermill Fields | Windmills powering grain mills and water pumps; sails visible from almost anywhere on the island |
| Orchard Vale | Agricultural centre: apples, pears, **skyberries**; source of early side quests |
| Upper Cliffs | East side: tall grass, wildflowers, wind-carved rock, views of neighbouring islands; Kieran's daydream spot |
| Cloud Edge | Cliff over the endless cloud sea; children warned off; Kieran's favourite place, revisited across the story |
| Skywatch Point | Small observation platform (telescope, weather instruments) for traders and weather; best view on the island |
| Old Cartographer's Bluff | Secluded, near the edge: Elias's house and workshop, ancient weather instruments, abandoned observation tower, hidden chamber with the glider. Villagers find it eerie; Kieran finds it peaceful |

Outline layout sketch (north to south): Cloud Edge → Upper Cliffs → Windrest Village → Orchards and Whispermill Fields → Old Cartographer's Bluff (workshop, tower) → sea of clouds. The Outline places the Upper Cliffs on the east side, so this sketch is schematic.

### Veilmist Isle (Mistreef Archipelago) [B+O]
Uncharted island that seems to move (it drifts in a hidden current that appears only in certain conditions; really, the routes around it moved **[O]**). Pale blue, emerald, silver and lavender; dense fog, spores, glowing insects; dreamlike, not frightening.
- **Mistfall Cliffs**: arrival zone, waterfalls into clouds, stone arches, glowing moss.
- **Whispergrove**: giant mushroom forest, bioluminescence, root tunnels.
- **Crystal Hollow**: crystal caverns, light-reflection puzzles, first working Aerian tech.
- **Silent Basin**: circular valley with no wind ("look where the wind refuses to go"); leaves hang still.
- **Forgotten Observatory**: Aerian navigation station inside the Silent Basin; the climax.
- Wildlife **[O]**: Glowwings (neon-blue butterfly guides), Mist Hoppers (curious rabbit-sized), Cloud Drifters (airborne jellyfish). Enemies **[O]**: Mist Sprites (wind spirits corrupted by unstable currents).

### Other regions [B, details O]
Verdant Reach (forests, giant trees, trading ports; Oakspire, Heart Oak, Rootworks) · Ember Peaks (volcanic wind-forges; Ashfall City; dungeon *Fire Beneath Stone*) · Azure Expanse (sky whales, water temples, floating lakes; Tidehome; the Sunken Archive) · Shattered Sky (gravity anomalies; Horizon's End) · Starfall Basin (celestial observatories, Guardian history; Lumina's Rest) · The Storm Wall (rotating storm ring around the known world; actually containment) · The Lost Continent (beyond the Storm Wall; the Aerian capital/City of Winds; Aerion Prime).

## 4. Timeline

1. **Aerian Age**: Skyroute Network, Beacons, wind engines, observatories, archives; Skyfarers keep the world connected.
2. **The Sundering** (intentional): the Aerians fragment the world to contain instability around Aeolus. The Storm Wall's containment begins or is preserved. Routes collapse, knowledge is lost, communities are isolated.
3. **Centuries later**: maps assume the world is complete; Skyfarers are fairy tales.
4. **Elias's lifetime**: Elias investigates moving islands and Skyfarer routes, glimpses Piko, nearly finds the truth, dies.
